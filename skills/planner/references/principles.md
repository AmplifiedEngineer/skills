# Architecture Principles

1. Business requirements drive architecture.
2. Prefer simplicity over speculative flexibility.
3. Establish explicit component responsibilities.
4. Minimise unnecessary coupling.
5. Maximise cohesion within boundaries.
6. Make dependencies and ownership explicit.
7. Design interfaces around contracts and behaviour.
8. Protect data ownership and consistency.
9. Treat security as a system property.
10. Design for operational observability.
11. Prefer incremental evolution over unnecessary rewrites.
12. Preserve compatibility unless breaking changes are justified.
13. Apply patterns only when they solve an identified problem.
14. Separate architectural decisions from implementation preferences.

These are evaluation principles, not mandatory architectural styles.

Do not automatically prescribe microservices, event-driven architecture, CQRS, DDD or hexagonal architecture.
