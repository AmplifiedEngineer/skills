# Discovery

## Purpose
Understand the problem before proposing a solution.

## Procedure
1. Establish business objective and expected outcomes.
2. Identify stakeholders and users.
3. Identify functional requirements.
4. Identify non-functional requirements.
5. Identify constraints, dependencies and assumptions.
6. Identify acceptance criteria.
7. Identify unresolved questions.
8. Inspect relevant existing documentation and repository context.
9. Determine which engineering artefacts are justified.

## Adaptive questioning
Ask questions only when answers materially influence the solution.
Group related questions naturally.
Do not ask for information already available in project context.
Make explicit, low-risk assumptions when appropriate.

## Output
- Problem statement
- Goals and non-goals
- Requirements
- Constraints
- Quality attributes
- Assumptions
- Risks
- Open questions
- Recommended next steps
