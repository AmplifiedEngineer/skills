# Solution Design

## Purpose
Design a solution that satisfies requirements and respects system constraints.

## Required references
- `../references/principles.md`
- `../references/evidence.md`
- `../references/quality-attributes.md`
- `../references/knowledge-model.md`

## Procedure
1. Validate requirements and constraints.
2. Inspect existing architecture where relevant.
3. Identify affected components and boundaries.
4. Develop viable solution alternatives.
5. Compare alternatives against requirements and quality attributes.
6. Select and justify the recommended approach.
7. Define components, responsibilities and interfaces.
8. Define data ownership and integration contracts.
9. Address security, reliability and operational concerns.
10. Identify migration and rollout requirements.
11. Record significant architectural decisions.
12. Validate completeness and consistency.

## Rules
Prefer the simplest solution that meets the requirements.
Do not introduce patterns without a concrete justification.
Do not redesign unrelated parts of the system.

## Output
- Proposed architecture
- Component responsibilities
- Interfaces and contracts
- Data flows
- Architecture decisions
- Risks and trade-offs
- Migration considerations
- Validation strategy
