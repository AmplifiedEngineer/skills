# Documentation Lifecycle

Create and maintain documents only when they provide lasting value or support a concrete engineering decision.

## Lifecycle actions

- GENERATE: create a needed document where none exists.
- UPDATE: correct or extend an existing authoritative document.
- KEEP: retain a document that remains accurate and useful.
- CONSOLIDATE: combine overlapping material while preserving useful history and links.
- DEPRECATE: mark superseded material and identify its replacement.
- SKIP: do not create a document when it would duplicate existing knowledge or add little value.
- DEFER: postpone work when ownership, evidence or a decision is unavailable.

## Selection

For each candidate document:
1. Identify its audience and purpose.
2. Check for an existing authoritative source.
3. Verify material claims against requirements, decisions and implementation.
4. Choose the smallest lifecycle action that resolves the need.
5. Preserve decision history and useful references.
6. Identify an owner or source of truth when known.

## Quality

Keep documents focused, current and easy to navigate.
Link related requirements, decisions, components and validation evidence.
Mark assumptions, unknowns and stale information.
Do not present proposed design as implemented fact.
