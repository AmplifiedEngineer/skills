# Context Acquisition

Gather relevant project information for the task at hand.

1. Identify the task, project, relevant time range and required output.
2. Discover available repositories, integrations and permitted tools.
3. Identify likely sources: requirements, issues, decisions, documentation, code and changes.
4. Prefer authoritative sources and follow links only as needed.
5. Retrieve minimum relevant records and capture canonical URLs or paths.
6. Record source dates, owners and status where available.
7. Reconcile conflicts and label verified facts, inferences and unknowns.
8. Prepare a context brief with ../templates/context-brief.md.

Do not imply access to unavailable systems. Do not copy secrets or unrelated private data into a brief. Report conflicting sources and the basis for assessing authority.
